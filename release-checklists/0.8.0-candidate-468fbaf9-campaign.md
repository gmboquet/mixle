## The pattern: a deferred limit came back blocking, for the second campaign running

The confirmed blocker, `GumbelEstimator`, was recorded in D-0202 as an acceptable stated limit
on the reasoning that it failed loudly. It does not: the verifier proved the data is fine by
showing the sibling `GeneralizedExtremeValueEstimator` fits the byte-identical shifted arrays
exactly across the whole offset range, where Gumbel is silently ~15x wrong at one offset and
raises an opaque internal error at others. `StudentTEstimator` -- never checked at all in the
previous wave -- returned a scale 1.6e8x too small at the Unix epoch with `converged=True` and
empty repairs. This closed the one-family-at-a-time strategy: the fix wave audited every
location-scale family in the tree for shift-equivariance rather than repairing only what the
next tester happened to catch. See D-0203 for the full account.
